## About me

I'm a Lead Software Architect and Platform Architect focused on modern software delivery: building
platforms and ways of working that help teams deliver faster, safer and with less operational
burden. I design and evolve CI/CD platforms and platform services: reusable pipeline templates and
components, developer feedback loops, onboarding patterns and governance that makes "the right way"
the easy way.

I work closely with product teams, security and operations to embed compliance and auditability
into the delivery flow without blocking delivery speed. I enjoy the intersection of architecture
and enablement: turning strategy and requirements into practical, adoptable standards that teams
actually want to use.
